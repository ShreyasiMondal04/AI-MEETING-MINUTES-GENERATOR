    Evaluation Formula & Methodology

    Word Error Rate (WER)

    $$\text{WER} = \frac{S + D + I}{N}$$

    Measures insertion ($I$), deletion ($D$), and substitution ($S$) errors across reference word count ($N$).

    Diarization Error Rate (DER)

    $$\text{DER} = \frac{\text{Miss} + \text{FalseAlarm} + \text{Confusion}}{\text{Total Vocal Time}}$$

    Quantifies speaker segmentation and attribution accuracy with optimal Hungarian permutation matching.

    ROUGE & BLEU

    $$\text{ROUGE-N} = \frac{\sum \text{Count}_{\text{match}}(\text{gram}_n)}{\sum \text{Count}(\text{gram}_n)}$$

    Measures semantic overlap and recall against human-written meeting minutes.
